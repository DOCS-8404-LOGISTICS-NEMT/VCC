RANGER FAMILIES, NOT BULLNOSES.

1983–1992

Early compact Ranger platform. Confirm engine and component variation before selecting service procedures.

1993–2011

Later compact Rangers encompass multiple styling and drivetrain changes; model year alone cannot establish part fitment.

2019–PRESENT

Modern North American midsize Ranger. Use model-year-specific factory information and diagnostics.

The 1980–1986 “Bullnose” label refers to Ford F-Series trucks, not the Ranger. For all generations, confirm VIN, drivetrain, engine and factory guidance.
