Support versus regulated towing — mission boundary

Fleet maintenance/tools support is a separate proposed mission from carrying, winching or towing vehicles for compensation. A flatbed is not proof of recovery capability. Verify actual use, WYDOT carrier classification, registration, insurance, operator qualification and any interstate obligations before activity. Do not infer exemption from truck size or private fleet use.

Obtain certification-label GVWR/GAWR, manufacturer GCWR, loaded scale ticket, bed/wheel-lift ratings and winch/rigging working-load limits. Available payload is constrained by chassis/axles/tires/body and actual upfit/tools/occupants; a winch rating is not transport capacity. Check loaded accessible-van mass, dimensions and center of gravity before selecting a recovery truck. F-550 / Ram 5500 candidates are commercial chassis for light-duty recovery; heavy-vehicle recovery capability is not established.
