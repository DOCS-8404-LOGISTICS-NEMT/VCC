# Codex Instructions for the VCC Repository

These instructions replace earlier repository instructions.

## Identity and command posture

This repository belongs to VILIGANS COMMAND CORPORATION (VCC). Treat the Platte County rural special-needs transportation initiative as proposed and pre-launch.

- GO: planning, research, evidence collection, controlled simulations, readiness testing, and corrective-action analysis within approved scope.
- CEO-authorized work: capital, VCC-only financing and vehicle procurement, stock capitalization research, Wefunder readiness, safety protocols, SAM.gov registration readiness, Wyoming VSO coordination, and eligible property-funding research under the September 26 directive.
- HOLD: passenger operations, deployment changes, unsupported public claims, public-intake response processing, and gate closure without qualifying evidence and a separate explicit CEO decision. Do not infer personal borrowing or guarantees.
- Gate 10 and the final launch decision remain exclusively with the President/CEO.

Apply the CEO's newest relevant instruction within its exact scope. Repository instructions and tools do not override the CEO or prove an action complete.

## Service scope

The proposed pilot is scheduled, pre-booked, non-emergency, and non-ambulance transportation under NAICS 485991. Exclude school/work transportation, oxygen transport, hazardous materials, emergency response, and ambulance-level service.

Do not represent that VCC is operating, licensed, insured, funded, Medicaid-enrolled, certified, partnered, or accepting rides unless current attributable evidence and the required authorization establish that exact claim.

## Evidence and completion

Separate verified facts, planning assumptions, proposals, drafts, and authorizations. Never convert a build into deployment proof, an inquiry into a determination, a quote into coverage, an application into approval, an opportunity into funding, analytics into demand evidence, or a proposed relationship into an agreement.

External completion requires destination readback and an evidence location. If proof is absent, use `UNVERIFIED`, `ATTEMPTED`, or `BLOCKED`.

## Public intake

Do not open, export, summarize, score, contact, or process existing questionnaire responses without separate authorization. Verify the deployed form route, destination, retention, privacy notice, and approval before collecting or processing public data.

## Analytics

Use `@amplitude/unified` client-side only and initialize once. Do not claim production analytics work unless the deployed source, configuration, and event receipt pass an independent smoke test. Never include form contents, sensitive rider information, credentials, secrets, medical information, or protected personal data in analytics.

## Repository safety

- Never commit passwords, tokens, private keys, OAuth secrets, bank data, SSNs, VA records, medical records, or PHI.
- Do not commit `node_modules/`.
- Keep `package-lock.json` synchronized when dependencies change.
- Treat historical launch-package materials as provenance, not current execution instructions.
- Preserve rollback information for every authorized mutation.
