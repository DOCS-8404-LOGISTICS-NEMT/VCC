## Compliance Notes

- Wyoming annual reports for corporations, LLCs, LPs, RLLPs, and statutory foundations are due on the first day of the entity's formation anniversary month.
- SAM.gov registration must be renewed every 365 days to remain active.
- SBA SDVOSB/VetCert requires 51%+ ownership and control by one or more service-disabled veterans; for a corporation, this must be supported by issued stock records and control documents.
- Wyoming operating authority is likely implicated for compensated intrastate passenger transportation; confirm directly with WYDOT before paid launch.
- Medicaid/NEMT revenue should not be assumed until the enrollment/billing route is verified with Wyoming Medicaid.
