# Wheatland Service Boundary And Route-Cost Model

VILIGANS COMMAND CORPORATION

Prepared: August 2, 2026

Status: Planning model only. Replace with broker-approved, authority-approved, vehicle-specific, mapped route quotes before external pricing, contracts, grants, lender packets, or paid service.

## Executive Summary

The Wheatland pilot should start with a tight service boundary, then expand only after insurance, authority, driver coverage, vehicle readiness, and partner demand are verified.

Recommended operating sequence:

1. Wheatland local medical and essential-service trips.
2. Wheatland plus nearby Platte County destinations by appointment block.
3. Limited specialty-care or intercity trips only after authority, insurance, driver hours, and pricing are confirmed.

## Service Boundary

| Tier | Boundary | Use | Launch decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Wheatland town limits | First test rides, partner intake, local appointments, senior/public-health destinations | Prepare first. |
| Tier 2 | Wheatland plus nearby Platte County communities | Scheduled ride windows, sponsor-supported blocks, senior/disability access | Hold until insurance and driver coverage assumptions are verified. |
| Tier 3 | Regional specialty-care trips outside Platte County | Higher-cost, higher-risk rides with more driver time and insurance exposure | Do not price publicly until authority and insurance path are confirmed. |

## Planning Cost Formula

Use these placeholders only for internal comparison:

| Cost component | Low planning value | High planning value | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Vehicle operating cost per mile | $1.25 | $2.25 | Fuel, maintenance, tires, depreciation, cleaning, accessibility reserve. |
| Driver / dispatch time per hour | $28 | $45 | Driver time, dispatch/admin, payroll taxes or contractor margin. |
| Insurance/compliance allocation per trip | $12 | $35 | Replace after broker quote. |
| Minimum trip administration cost | $15 | $35 | Intake, confirmation, reminder, reporting, cancellation/no-show handling. |
| Contingency | 12% | 20% | Weather, rural deadhead, wait time, repairs, missed connections. |

Internal pricing should never be lower than the full cost model plus reserve.

## Initial Rate-Card Structure For Approval

Do not publish rates yet. Use this structure for board/partner review:

| Offer | Draft structure | Approval note |
|---|---|---|
| Anchor readiness fee | Monthly reserved-capacity fee for dispatch readiness, reporting, route planning, and partner coordination | Quote after insurance/authority assumptions. |
| Local scheduled trip | Base pickup fee plus per-mile and wait-time charge | Do not underprice short trips. |
| Ride block | Prepaid block of local Wheatland rides with defined eligibility and reporting | Good sponsor product. |
| Partner referral package | Readiness fee plus per-trip charges for partner-referred riders | Best first healthcare anchor offer. |
| Regional trip | Higher base fee, mileage, wait time, cancellation rule, and driver-hour reserve | Hold until authority/insurance confirmation. |

## Data To Collect Before Pricing

1. Exact first vehicle class, capacity, accessibility equipment, fuel type, and expected maintenance profile.
2. Insurance premium, deductible, exclusions, driver requirements, and territory restrictions.
3. WYDOT/FMCSA authority determination.
4. Driver wage or contractor rate, backup-driver availability, and overtime rules.
5. Partner trip windows and expected no-show/cancellation profile.
6. Whether riders need ambulatory, wheelchair, door-through-door, caregiver, or special-assistance service.
7. Weather and road-condition restrictions.

## Stop/Go Gates

No external rate card until:

- Insurance broker confirms bindable coverage assumptions.
- WYDOT/FMCSA path is documented.
- Vehicle and driver assumptions are known.
- Partner scope is approved.
- Privacy and rider-data handling rules are approved.
