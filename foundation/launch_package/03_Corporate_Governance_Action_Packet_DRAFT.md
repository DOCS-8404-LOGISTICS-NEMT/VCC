The corporation authorizes preparation of grant, sponsorship, subrecipient, and operator materials but prohibits final submission or binding commitments until officer/board approval.

### Resolution 005 - Transportation Launch Gate

The corporation prohibits paid passenger service until:

- Authority determination/application is complete
- Insurance is bound

- Driver files are complete
- Operating policies are approved
- Incident and privacy processes are in place
- Service area and rate card are approved
