## Conditions And Safeguards

This commitment is conditional until all required approvals are complete.

VCC will not:

- Start paid passenger transportation before launch gates clear.
- Treat a pledge as available cash until funds are received or a binding written commitment is approved.
- Accept restricted funds that conflict with insurance, lender, grant, sponsor, public-sector, or operating-authority rules.
- Use sponsor funds for personal expenses or unrelated projects.
- Publish sponsor name/logo without approval.
- Collect or store PHI or detailed rider medical information without approved privacy controls.
