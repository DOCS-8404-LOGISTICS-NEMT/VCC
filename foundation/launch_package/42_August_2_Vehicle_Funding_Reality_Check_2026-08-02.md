## Bottom Line

Funding within the week is realistic only if VCC defines "funding" as one or more of these near-term proofs:

- Written sponsor/readiness pledge.
- Approved founder/company bridge authorization.
- Commercial lease or lender preapproval/term sheet.
- Confirmed partner payment path or purchase-order path.

Grant cash within the week is not realistic. Grants remain useful for scale-up, match, sustainability, and credibility, but they should not be treated as the source for August 9 funding proof or August 15 vehicle possession.

Pilot vehicle possession by mid-month is realistic only through one of these lanes:

1. Commercial lease path with fast preliminary fit, assigned vehicle, total due at delivery, and insurance bindability.
2. Short-term accessible rental bridge for dry runs, demos, route checks, and partner readiness.
3. Low-cost used accessible vehicle purchase only if the written quote, inspection, insurance, and financing/down-payment stack close quickly.
4. Approved partner vehicle only if the partner has vehicle, driver, insurance, authority, dispatch, payment, data, and liability roles in writing.
