# WYDOT, Insurance, And Vehicle Readiness Packet

Draft status: Internal preparation only.

## Objective

Create a clean stop/go process before VCC or DOCS 8404 provides any paid passenger transportation.

## Launch Gate

Do not launch paid passenger service until all are complete:

- Wyoming operating authority determination or application is complete
- USDOT/FMCSA applicability is documented
- Commercial insurance is bound
- Vehicle title/registration/lease authority is documented
- Vehicle is inspected and appropriate for rider profile
- Wheelchair-accessible capability is available before wheelchair/special-needs commitments
- Driver file is complete
- Policies are approved
- Service area and rate card are approved

## WYDOT Operating Authority Questions

Ask WYDOT:

1. Does VCC or DOCS 8404 need Wyoming intrastate operating authority for scheduled non-emergency passenger transportation for compensation?
2. Should the applicant be VCC, DOCS 8404, or both?
3. Is MC-100 the correct application?
4. Is Form E insurance filing required?
5. Is a USDOT number required for the proposed vehicle type/capacity/service model?
6. Are there special rules for contract transportation, Medicaid/NEMT, senior/disability rides, or wheelchair-accessible vans?
7. Are there county/local permits or inspections required?
8. What insurance minimums apply for the specific passenger capacity and service type?
9. Can VCC operate as a contractor/subrecipient under another eligible provider's authority, or must it hold its own?
10. What records must be kept for driver qualification, safety, inspections, and complaints?

## Insurance Broker Request Summary

Entity: VILIGANS COMMAND CORPORATION and/or DOCS 8404 Logistics & NEMT LLC, final insured to be confirmed.

Service model: scheduled, non-emergency, non-ambulance passenger transportation for rural Wyoming riders, including seniors, individuals with disabilities, veterans, special-needs riders, medical appointment access, partner-referred trips, private-pay trips, and sponsored ride blocks.

Initial operations: limited pilot geography, scheduled trips only, no emergency response, no medical treatment, no stretcher transport unless separately approved and insured.

Coverage requested:

- Commercial auto
- General liability
- Professional liability / errors and omissions
- Abuse and molestation coverage
- Workers compensation
- Umbrella / excess liability
- Cyber/privacy coverage for trip and referral information
- Hired and non-owned auto if applicable

## Vehicle Requirements

Preferred first pilot vehicle:

- Wheelchair-accessible van or small bus
- Commercial-grade ramp or lift
- Wheelchair securement system
- Passenger restraint system
- High roof or accessible entry where needed
- Heating/air conditioning suitable for Wyoming conditions
- Winter tires or traction plan
- GPS/dispatch tracking
- Backup camera
- Fire extinguisher and first-aid kit
- Daily inspection checklist
- Maintenance records

Avoid:

- Launching special-needs passenger service using an unsuitable pickup truck
- Using any vehicle for NEMT passenger service without regulatory, insurance, ADA, inspection, and operating suitability review
- Titling or leasing vehicles through an unverified private ownership or statutory-foundation structure for grant-funded operations without checking grant title rules

## Vehicle Acquisition Strategy

Ranked options:

1. Lease or purchase one used wheelchair-accessible van after insurance quote and authority path are confirmed.
2. Partner with an existing provider for vehicle access during soft launch.
3. Use sponsored capital for down payment or lease deposit.
4. Seek WYDOT/FTA vehicle funding next cycle through eligible applicant/subrecipient structure.
5. Use non-accessible vehicle only for non-wheelchair, low-assistance trips if permitted, insured, and operationally safe.
