# NEMT And Rural Special-Needs Transportation Operations Manual

Draft status: Internal operating draft. Not approved for launch use.

## Service Scope

VCC/DOCS 8404 will provide scheduled, non-emergency, non-ambulance transportation only after all launch gates are complete.

The pilot will not provide:

- Emergency medical transportation
- Ambulance service
- Medical treatment
- Stretcher transport unless separately approved, insured, and authorized
- Medicaid billing unless enrollment and billing rules are confirmed
- Unscheduled emergency response

## Pilot Service Model

- Scheduled trips only
- Limited Wyoming pilot geography
- Partner-referred and private-pay trips
- Senior, disability, veteran, special-needs, and healthcare-access focus
- No guarantee of same-day service during pilot unless approved
- Wheelchair trips only after vehicle and driver securement readiness are confirmed

## Rider Intake

Collect only what is necessary:

- Rider name
- Contact information
- Pickup/drop-off locations
- Appointment date/time
- Mobility needs
- Wheelchair or assistive device needs
- Companion/caregiver details if applicable
- Emergency contact
- Payment/partner referral source
- Permission to coordinate trip logistics with referring partner

Do not collect unnecessary medical diagnosis information.

## Scheduling And Dispatch

1. Confirm rider eligibility and service area.
2. Confirm trip purpose is non-emergency.
3. Confirm pickup/drop-off details.
4. Confirm mobility/accessibility needs.
5. Confirm payment or partner authorization.
6. Assign driver and vehicle.
7. Send confirmation.
8. Track trip status.
9. Record completion, cancellation, no-show, delay, incident, and mileage.

## Driver Requirements

Before transporting riders, each driver file must include:

- Driver application
- Valid license
- MVR review
- Background check
- Drug/alcohol policy acknowledgment
- CPR/First Aid plan or certificate
- Passenger assistance training
- Wheelchair securement training if applicable
- Defensive driving/winter driving training
- Insurance approval
- Confidentiality/privacy acknowledgment
- Incident reporting training

## Rider Assistance Rules

Drivers may:

- Provide door-to-door or curb-to-curb assistance as approved
- Assist with seat belts and securement
- Help riders safely enter/exit the vehicle within training limits
- Coordinate with caregivers or facility staff

Drivers may not:

- Provide medical treatment
- Lift riders beyond approved training/equipment limits
- Enter private residences except under a written, approved assistance policy
- Handle medication
- Accept cash tips unless policy allows
- Make medical judgments

## ADA And Accessibility

The pilot must provide truthful service descriptions and avoid promising accessible capacity before the accessible vehicle, securement equipment, and trained drivers are ready.

Accessibility controls:

- Wheelchair securement checklist
- Service animal policy
- Reasonable accommodation request log
- Complaint process
- Staff training log
- Equivalent-service review if non-accessible vehicles are used for any demand-responsive service

## Privacy And Trip Data

Trip data is operationally sensitive.

Rules:

- Collect minimum necessary information.
- Store trip records in approved systems only.
- Do not share rider information without permission or legal/contractual authority.
- Do not include diagnosis details in general dispatch notes unless operationally necessary.
- Report privacy incidents immediately.
- Use secure email or portal methods when required by partner agreement.

## No-Show And Cancellation

Draft policy:

- Rider cancellation requested at least 24 hours before scheduled pickup where practical.
- Same-day cancellation documented with reason.
- No-show occurs when rider is unavailable after driver arrives and approved waiting period expires.
- Repeated no-shows trigger partner/rider review, not automatic abandonment of medically necessary recurring trips.

## Weather And Safety

Operations may be delayed or cancelled due to:

- Severe weather
- Road closure
- Vehicle defect
- Driver safety concern
- Rider condition creating non-emergency transport risk
- Insurance/authority limitation

Decision authority: operations lead or designated officer.

## Incident Reporting

Report and document:

- Collision
- Rider fall
- Injury or complaint of injury
- Vehicle defect
- Securement failure
- Abuse/neglect concern
- Privacy incident
- Passenger behavior/safety issue
- Missed critical appointment
- Law enforcement interaction

Incident file should include date/time, driver, vehicle, rider, location, description, witnesses, photos if appropriate, immediate action, notifications, and follow-up.

## Vehicle Maintenance

Required records:

- Daily inspection
- Mileage
- Fuel
- Maintenance
- Repairs
- Tire condition
- Lift/ramp operation
- Securement equipment
- Cleaning/sanitation
- Defect correction

## Pilot KPIs

Track weekly:

- Trips completed
- Trips denied
- No-shows
- Cancellations
- On-time pickup rate
- On-time drop-off rate
- Miles driven
- Revenue
- Cost per trip
- Fuel cost
- Maintenance issues
- Rider satisfaction
- Partner referrals
- Accessibility requests
- Incidents
