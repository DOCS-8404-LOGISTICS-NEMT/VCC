# 90-Day Wyoming Pilot Budget

Draft status: planning estimate only. Replace assumptions with quotes before lender, grant, or board use.

## Budget Assumptions

- One pilot service area
- One accessible passenger vehicle
- Scheduled non-emergency trips only
- Limited operating days during soft launch
- One primary driver plus backup driver
- One dispatcher/operations role, part time at launch
- No Medicaid revenue assumed until enrollment/billing path is confirmed

## Fastest Revenue Offer

Offer partners a 90-day pilot with:

- Fixed monthly availability/readiness fee
- Per-trip mileage or flat zone charge
- Weekly reporting
- Limited number of reserved trip windows
- Clear cancellation/no-show policy
- No medical treatment or emergency transport
- Compliance/insurance documents provided after approval
