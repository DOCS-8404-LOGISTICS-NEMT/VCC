# Corporate Bylaws Draft

VILIGANS COMMAND CORPORATION

Wyoming Filing ID: 2025-001851867

Draft date: August 1, 2026

Status: Draft for review. Not adopted until approved and signed by the corporation.

## Article 1 - Corporate Identity

1.1 Name. The name of the corporation is VILIGANS COMMAND CORPORATION.

1.2 Jurisdiction. The corporation is a Wyoming profit corporation.

1.3 Filing Date. The corporation was filed with the Wyoming Secretary of State on December 26, 2025.

1.4 Duration. The corporation has perpetual duration unless dissolved according to law.

1.5 Principal Office. The current principal office shown in the Wyoming filing record is 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, unless changed by the corporation through proper update procedures.

1.6 Registered Agent. The corporation shall maintain a Wyoming registered agent and registered office as required by Wyoming law.

1.7 Purpose. The corporation may engage in any lawful business purpose, including rural transportation, special-needs transportation, NEMT readiness, logistics, mobility partnerships, grant-supported operations, sponsorships, and related business activities.

## Article 2 - Shares And Ownership Records

2.1 Authorized Shares. The corporation is authorized to issue 1,000,000 common shares with a par value of $0.001 per share and 0 preferred shares, according to the reviewed Wyoming filing information.

2.2 Issuance. Shares may be issued only by board approval or valid written consent.

2.3 Consideration. The board shall document the consideration received for any issued shares.

2.4 Stock Ledger. The corporation shall maintain a stock ledger showing shareholder name, address, share class, number of shares, issue date, consideration, certificate or uncertificated notice reference, transfer restrictions, and any cancellation or transfer.

2.5 Transfer Restrictions. No share transfer is valid unless approved according to these bylaws, board resolution, applicable securities law, and any shareholder agreement or transfer restriction.

2.6 VetCert Control. If the corporation seeks SBA VetCert or SDVOSB readiness, the corporation shall maintain records showing ownership and control by the qualifying service-disabled veteran before making any certification claim.

## Article 3 - Shareholders

3.1 Annual Meeting. The corporation shall hold an annual shareholder meeting or approve annual shareholder action by written consent.

3.2 Special Meetings. Special meetings may be called by the president, board, or shareholders holding the voting power required by law or applicable agreements.

3.3 Written Consent. Any action that may be taken at a shareholder meeting may be taken by written consent where permitted by law.

3.4 Voting. Each common share is entitled to one vote unless the Articles, board-approved share terms, or applicable law provide otherwise.

3.5 Records. The corporation shall preserve shareholder consents, meeting minutes, notices, waivers, and voting records in the corporate binder.

## Article 4 - Board Of Directors

4.1 Authority. The business and affairs of the corporation shall be managed by or under the direction of the board of directors.

4.2 Number. The number of directors shall be set by the board or shareholders and recorded in the corporate binder.

4.3 Term. Directors serve until their successors are elected and qualified, or until resignation, removal, incapacity, or death.

4.4 Meetings. Board meetings may be held in person, by telephone, by video conference, or by other reliable communication method.

4.5 Written Consent. Board action may be taken by written consent where permitted by law.

4.6 Quorum. A majority of directors then in office shall constitute a quorum unless a different rule is adopted by valid resolution or required by law.

4.7 Standard Of Conduct. Directors shall act in good faith, with care, and in a manner reasonably believed to be in the best interests of the corporation.

4.8 Conflict Review. Directors and officers shall disclose related-party, financial, family, trust, statutory foundation, vehicle, lender, vendor, and contract conflicts before approval of affected transactions.

## Article 5 - Officers

5.1 Offices. The corporation may appoint a president, chief executive officer, secretary, treasurer, and any other officers approved by the board.

5.2 President / CEO. The president or chief executive officer manages ordinary business affairs subject to board direction and these bylaws.

5.3 Secretary. The secretary maintains corporate records, minutes, consents, notices, officer/director records, and the corporate binder.

5.4 Treasurer. The treasurer oversees financial records, bank documentation, budgets, lender files, grant financial records, and financial reporting.

5.5 Authority Limits. No officer may bind the corporation to financing, insurance, vehicle acquisition, stock issuance, grant submission, regulatory filing, contract, MOU, or paid passenger launch unless authorized by board resolution or written consent.

## Article 6 - Subsidiaries And Operating Units

6.1 Operating Subsidiary. DOCS 8404 Logistics & NEMT LLC may be used as an operating subsidiary only after state formation, ownership, management authority, and tax/accounting treatment are verified.

6.2 Intercompany Agreements. Any services, vehicle use, equipment use, IP use, reimbursement, management, or lease arrangement between VCC and any subsidiary or related party must be documented in writing.

6.3 Separate Records. The corporation shall maintain separate records, bank accounts, contracts, insurance files, and operating documents where needed to preserve entity separateness.

## Article 7 - Transportation Launch Controls

7.1 No Paid Passenger Launch Without Approval. The corporation shall not launch paid passenger transportation until operating authority, insurance, vehicle readiness, driver files, policies, privacy controls, and incident procedures are approved.

7.2 NEMT Limitation. The corporation shall not bill Medicaid or represent itself as an enrolled Medicaid/NEMT provider unless provider enrollment and billing authority are verified.

7.3 ADA And Safety. The corporation shall maintain ADA-related operating procedures, wheelchair securement procedures where applicable, driver training, vehicle inspections, and incident reporting.

## Article 8 - Banking, Finance, Grants, And Contracts

8.1 Bank Accounts. Bank accounts may be opened only with proper officer authority and required corporate records.

8.2 Borrowing. Borrowing, credit facilities, pledges, guarantees, and security interests require board approval.

8.3 Grants. Grant applications, subrecipient agreements, sponsorship agreements, and federal registrations require review and approval before submission.

8.4 Contracts. Contracts, MOUs, service agreements, vehicle leases, insurance binders, and vendor agreements require authorized approval and signature.

## Article 9 - Corporate Records

9.1 Records To Maintain. The corporation shall maintain Articles, filing receipts, EIN proof, bylaws, consents, minutes, stock ledger, cap table, tax records, annual reports, good-standing records, contracts, insurance, licenses, grant files, and transportation compliance files.

9.2 Sensitive Records. Veteran, disability, medical, tax, bank, SSN, VA, and other private records shall be stored securely and redacted before broad distribution.

9.3 Source-Proof Register. The corporation shall maintain a source-proof register identifying whether each key document is draft, signed, filed, accepted, expired, superseded, or missing.

## Article 10 - Amendments

These bylaws may be amended by the board or shareholders according to Wyoming law, the Articles, and any valid shareholder agreements.

## Adoption

These bylaws are not effective until adopted by the corporation.
