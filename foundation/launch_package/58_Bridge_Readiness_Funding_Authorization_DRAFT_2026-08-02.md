## Fast Decision Use Cases

### Rental Bridge

Use this path if an accessible rental can be available by August 15 and VCC needs approved funds to preserve the option after insurance and authority screens.

Minimum proof before obligation approval:

- Rental class and availability.
- Total due and due date.
- Insurance requirements.
- Business-use and dry-run/demo permission.
- Pickup/delivery plan.
- Cancellation terms.
- Authority and insurance notes.

### Vehicle Purchase Or Lease

Use this path if a specific accessible passenger vehicle or commercial lease unit is available and likely to be lost without rapid approval.

Minimum proof before obligation approval:

- VIN or vehicle class.
- Written price or total due.
- Title/brand or lease term notes.
- Inspection/service/warranty information.
- Commercial-use permission.
- Insurance path.
- Financing, bridge, or down-payment source.

### Insurance Deposit

Use this path if a broker/carrier confirms bindability and gives a firm requirement list.

Minimum proof before obligation approval:

- Carrier or broker name.
- Coverage lines.
- Limits.
- Driver and vehicle conditions.
- Form E capability if required.
- Premium/deposit amount.
- Exclusions.
- Bind date and documents required.
