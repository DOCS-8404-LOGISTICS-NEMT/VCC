## VCC/DOCS Responsibilities

Subject to authority, insurance, vehicle, staffing, and approval gates, VCC/DOCS will:

- Maintain operating policies
- Maintain required insurance
- Maintain driver qualification files
- Maintain vehicle inspection/maintenance records
- Schedule and dispatch approved trips
- Track trip data and pilot performance
- Provide weekly or monthly pilot reports
- Report incidents as required
- Protect rider information

## Partner Responsibilities

Partner will:

- Identify eligible referral categories
- Provide referral contacts
- Confirm payment/funding route where applicable
- Avoid sharing unnecessary medical information
- Coordinate rider communication as agreed
- Provide pilot feedback
- Consider a letter of support or grant partnership where appropriate

## No Emergency Or Medical Care

The parties acknowledge that the pilot is not ambulance service, emergency medical transportation, medical treatment, or clinical care.

## Insurance And Compliance

No trips will begin until required authority, insurance, vehicle, driver, and operating controls are complete.

## Privacy

The parties will exchange only the minimum necessary trip coordination information unless a separate privacy, data-sharing, HIPAA business associate, or similar agreement is required and approved.

## Termination

Either party may terminate the MOU with written notice. Any paid service agreement should include separate payment, insurance, indemnity, compliance, and termination terms.

## Non-Binding Status

Unless expressly stated in a separate signed agreement, this MOU is intended as a planning and pilot-readiness document and does not obligate either party to pay funds, provide trips, submit grants, or enter a final contract.
