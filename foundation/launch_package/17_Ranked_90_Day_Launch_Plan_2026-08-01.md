# Ranked 90-Day Launch Plan

VILIGANS COMMAND CORPORATION

Wyoming Rural Special-Needs Transportation Pilot

Prepared: August 1, 2026

Status: Draft execution plan. Do not treat this as authority to file, sign, submit, bind insurance, issue stock, purchase/lease vehicles, or launch paid passenger service.

## Strategic Conclusion

The fastest path to a funded pilot is not a grant-only path. VCC should secure one anchor partner or sponsor commitment first, then use that commitment to support insurance, vehicle acquisition, operating authority, local sponsorship, lender discussions, and grant applications.

Recommended pilot structure:

`VILIGANS COMMAND CORPORATION -> DOCS 8404 Logistics & NEMT LLC after LLC filing/status is verified`

Current source-proof status:

- VCC Wyoming corporation evidence is verified.
- DOCS 8404 official Wyoming LLC filing proof is still needed before it is used as the active operating subsidiary in binding external documents.
- VCC governance records, stock ledger, cap table, SAM/UEI, insurance, WYDOT authority, vehicle readiness, and driver files remain launch gates.

## Fastest Path To A Funded Pilot

Within 14 days:

1. Build the Wheatland / Platte County service boundary and route-cost model.
2. Confirm WYDOT operating authority path.
3. Request commercial NEMT/passenger insurance quotes.
4. Select one accessible passenger vehicle option.
5. Run the seven-day funding sprint for pilot vehicle deposit, insurance deposit, readiness-fee, sponsor, and financing commitments.
6. Send controlled outreach to 20 anchor partners and 20 sponsors.
7. Decide whether FTA ICAM can be pursued directly or through an eligible lead partner before September 9, 2026.

Within 30 days:

1. Secure one signed LOI/MOU, sponsor pledge, or paid pilot commitment.
2. Complete VCC governance and ownership/control records.
3. Complete SAM.gov/UEI and Grants.gov readiness if grant/federal path is pursued.
4. Prepare vehicle, driver, policy, rate-card, and launch-gate files.

Within 60 days:

1. Bind insurance only after approval.
2. File operating authority only after approval.
3. Execute vehicle lease/purchase only after approval.
4. Train drivers and run internal test trips.
5. Finalize pilot reporting dashboard.

Within 90 days:

1. Start a controlled soft launch only if all gates are complete.
2. Collect weekly data: trips, miles, denials, no-shows, on-time rate, cost per trip, rider need, and partner source.
3. Convert pilot evidence into WYCF, WYDOT next-cycle, VA HRTG partner, sponsor, lender, and local-government funding packages.

## Operational Requirements

- Approved service area and trip types
- Dispatch procedure
- Rider intake form
- Partner referral form
- Rate card
- Cancellation/no-show policy
- Incident reporting policy
- Weather cancellation policy
- ADA/reasonable accommodation policy
- Wheelchair securement procedure
- Privacy/trip-data handling policy
- Maintenance and daily vehicle inspection logs
- Weekly KPI reporting

## Vehicle Requirements

Preferred first vehicle:

- Wheelchair-accessible van or small bus
- Ramp/lift and securement system
- Commercial passenger/NEMT insurance eligibility
- Good winter-weather suitability
- Heat/AC, backup camera, GPS/dispatch tracking
- Daily inspection and maintenance records
- Proper title/lease/registration

Do not use any unsuitable vehicle as the primary special-needs transportation vehicle without specific insurance, regulatory, ADA, passenger-safety, and operating suitability review.

## Staffing Requirements

Minimum pilot team:

- Executive/operator lead
- Part-time dispatcher/operations coordinator
- Primary driver
- Backup driver
- Compliance/admin support
- Insurance/legal/CPA advisors as needed

Driver file requirements:

- Valid license
- MVR check
- Background check
- Insurance approval
- Drug/alcohol policy acknowledgment
- CPR/First Aid plan or certificate
- Passenger assistance training
- Wheelchair securement training if applicable
- Defensive/winter driving training
- Incident reporting and privacy acknowledgment

## Compliance Tasks

P0 compliance gates:

1. Verify DOCS 8404 LLC state filing or use VCC-only interim path.
2. Complete VCC bylaws, initial consent, officer appointments, stock ledger, cap table, and signing authority.
3. Confirm SAM.gov/UEI and Grants.gov access.
4. Confirm WYDOT operating authority path.
5. Confirm USDOT/FMCSA applicability.
6. Obtain commercial insurance quotes and bind only after approval.
7. Select and inspect vehicle.
8. Approve operating manual, ADA, privacy, incident, and maintenance policies.
9. Confirm Medicaid/NEMT enrollment and billing route before assuming Medicaid revenue.

## Business Risks

1. Launching before authority/insurance/vehicle/driver gates are complete.
2. Using DOCS 8404 externally before official LLC status is verified.
3. Claiming SDVOSB, Medicaid, WYDOT, or insurance status before approval.
4. Underpricing trips and running out of cash.
5. Relying on grants without anchor revenue.
6. Using unverified private ownership, statutory foundation, or vehicle ownership language inconsistently.
7. Mishandling medical trip or veteran evidence.
8. Launching with one driver and no backup vehicle/coverage.
