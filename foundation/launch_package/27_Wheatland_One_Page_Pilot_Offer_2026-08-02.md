# Wheatland 90-Day Pilot Offer

VILIGANS COMMAND CORPORATION

Prepared: August 2, 2026

Status: Draft for approval. Do not send externally until claims, service boundary, insurance language, authority language, and contact list are approved.

## Offer Name

Wheatland Rural Access Pilot

## Purpose

VILIGANS COMMAND CORPORATION is preparing a controlled Wheatland / Platte County transportation-access pilot for scheduled, non-emergency, non-ambulance trips. The pilot is designed to document unmet transportation need, reduce missed access to healthcare and essential services, and create a fundable operating model for rural mobility support.

## Intended Riders

- Seniors.
- Individuals with disabilities or special-needs transportation barriers.
- Veterans.
- Residents needing scheduled healthcare-access or essential-service transportation.
- Partner-referred riders within approved eligibility and service limits.

## What VCC Is Asking From The First Anchor Partner

One of the following:

1. Letter of support confirming transportation-access need.
2. Referral workflow planning call.
3. Monthly readiness fee for reserved planning and launch capacity.
4. Sponsor-funded ride block for defined local trips after operating gates clear.
5. Pilot MOU after legal, insurance, and authority review.

## What VCC Will Provide After Launch Gates Clear

- Scheduled, non-emergency, non-ambulance transportation within the approved Wheatland / Platte County service boundary.
- Weekly dashboard: ride requests, completed rides, denied rides, unmet need, trip purpose category, cost per trip, incidents, and partner referrals.
- Controlled dispatch and intake process.
- Driver, vehicle, insurance, and operating documentation after approval.
- Clear no-emergency, no-medical-treatment, no-stretcher, and no-unscheduled-response limits.

## What VCC Will Not Claim Yet

VCC will not represent that it is currently:

- WYDOT-authorized.
- Medicaid-enrolled.
- Commercially insured for passenger service.
- Operating a public ride service.
- Certified by SBA VetCert or any other program unless source-proofed.
- Ready to transport riders before all launch gates clear.

## Pilot Boundary

Initial planning boundary:

- Wheatland town limits.
- Nearby Platte County destinations only after route, driver, insurance, and authority assumptions are confirmed.
- Regional trips only after separate pricing and risk review.

## Partner Discussion Agenda

1. What transportation barriers are you seeing in Wheatland / Platte County?
2. Which destinations and trip types create the most unmet need?
3. What days/times would be most useful?
4. What rider assistance levels are common?
5. What reporting would help justify sponsors, grants, or local support?
6. Would your organization consider a support letter, referral workflow, sponsor pledge, readiness fee, or MOU?

## First 30-Day Goal

Secure one written partner signal:

- support letter,
- MOU draft review,
- sponsor-funded ride block discussion,
- readiness-fee discussion, or
- documented local unmet-need statement.
