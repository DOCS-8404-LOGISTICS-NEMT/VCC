## Executive Answer

If VCC cannot prove rental, lease, purchase, insurance, and authority paths fast enough, the best remaining way to preserve an August 15 pilot vehicle target is a partner-vehicle path.

The strongest local public-source candidate is Platte ParaTransit because its public materials describe a Wheatland-based nonprofit medical transportation service for Platte County residents, including a small fleet with a wheelchair-accessible vehicle. Services for Seniors is also relevant because its public materials describe bus/van transportation in Wheatland and Platte County, with scheduled dispatch and published rider rules.

Neither candidate proves that VCC may use, dispatch, co-brand, fund, subcontract, borrow, reserve, or claim access to a vehicle. The partner path counts only if written proof confirms the vehicle, driver, insurance, authority, scheduling, payment, data, liability, permitted use, and approval sequence.

## Partner Vehicle Models

| Model | What It Could Preserve | Minimum Proof Required |
|---|---|---|
| Referral and coordination partner | VCC can validate Wheatland demand and build referral workflows while partner continues its own transportation operations. | Written role split, no VCC dispatch/control claim, no rider data exchange beyond approved minimum-necessary information, and no service availability claim by VCC. |
| Partner-operated demonstration capacity | Partner uses its own vehicle/driver/insurance for approved demos, dry runs, or readiness events. | Written event scope, vehicle/driver proof, insurance, authority or exemption position, safety responsibilities, and no paid passenger launch claim. |
| Subcontracted or service-agreement capacity | Partner provides trips under a written agreement after authority, insurance, privacy, payment, and indemnity review. | Signed agreement, payment terms, insurance certificates, authority proof, driver controls, trip documentation, privacy controls, and exact obligation approval. |
| Support-letter and grant partner | Partner validates vehicle need and supports grants/funding while VCC pursues its own vehicle. | Written support letter, no vehicle-control claim unless separately documented, allowed-use language, and grant/subrecipient role clarity. |
| Vehicle-capacity bridge | Partner supplies limited vehicle capacity while VCC pursues rental, lease, or purchase. | Written schedule capacity, vehicle class, accessibility, driver, insurance, authority, maintenance, cost, restrictions, and approval before any public or paid use. |

## Stop Rules

- A signed MOU, service agreement, or subcontract.

- Rider names, medical details, Medicaid status, VA records, diagnosis, trip purpose tied to treatment, or other sensitive data.
- Vehicle dispatch, trip scheduling, passenger pickup, public launch announcement, or proof of service.

## Evidence Standard

Partner vehicle proof counts toward August 15 only if the file has written proof or dated call notes covering:

1. Partner legal name and decision-maker.
2. Vehicle class and accessibility equipment.
3. Driver and training responsibility.
4. Insurance policy type, limits, certificate path, and exclusions.
5. Authority or exemption position for the exact role.
6. Scheduling and dispatch responsibility.
7. Payment, reimbursement, donation, or no-cost terms.
8. Rider data and privacy boundaries.
9. Liability, incidents, maintenance, and safety responsibility.
10. Public-use and public-claims limits.
11. Required board/officer/agency approval before anything becomes binding.
