# VILIGANS COMMAND CORPORATION

## Executive Summary

VILIGANS COMMAND CORPORATION is building DOCS 8404 Logistics & NEMT LLC as a Wyoming-focused rural mobility and non-emergency medical transportation operating platform. The mission is to improve access to scheduled, non-emergency transportation for rural residents, seniors, individuals with disabilities, special-needs riders, veterans, and patients who need reliable access to healthcare, community services, employment, and essential appointments.

VCC's launch strategy is to start with a controlled Wyoming pilot: limited geography, scheduled trips only, commercial insurance, documented driver qualification, accessibility procedures, partner referrals, and weekly performance reporting. The initial pilot should prioritize high-need trip categories such as dialysis, primary care, behavioral health, disability services, senior services, veteran care, pharmacy access, and county/community health referrals.

## Current Objective

Launch a compliant, contract-backed rural special-needs transportation pilot within 90 days.

## Fastest Path To A Funded Pilot

The fastest path is an anchor partner arrangement before relying on grant awards. VCC should secure one written LOI, MOU, paid pilot agreement, or sponsored ride-block commitment from a hospital, clinic, county agency, senior center, disability provider, VSO, employer, church/civic organization, or local sponsor. That partner commitment can then support insurance, vehicle financing/leasing, operating authority readiness, grant applications, and lender underwriting.

## Core Service Model

- Scheduled, non-emergency passenger transportation
- Rural and small-community service focus
- Special-needs, senior, disability, veteran, and healthcare-access trips
- Wheelchair-accessible vehicle pathway before special-needs launch
- Partner-referred and private-pay trips
- No ambulance, emergency medical care, or unscheduled emergency response
- No Medicaid billing unless enrollment and billing authority are confirmed

## Launch Requirements

- Official Wyoming entity records and good standing
- Signed governance and ownership/control records
- Active SAM.gov/UEI for federal funding readiness
- WYDOT operating authority determination or application
- Commercial NEMT/passenger transportation insurance
- ADA-accessible vehicle acquisition or lease plan
- Driver qualification and training files
- Operating manual, incident process, privacy policy, and maintenance records
- Anchor partner LOI/MOU or pilot service agreement
- 90-day budget and 12-month cash forecast

## Funding Strategy

The primary funding stack should combine:

1. Anchor service agreement or sponsorship
2. Local sponsor support and ride-block funding
3. Wyoming Community Foundation fall-cycle preparation
4. WYDOT/FTA 5310, 5311, and 5339 readiness through eligible applicant/subrecipient paths
5. VA HRTG next-cycle readiness through a qualified VSO or State Veterans Service Agency lead
6. SBA VetCert readiness for future SDVOSB contracting
7. Bank/lender package after partner LOI, insurance quote, vehicle plan, and operating budget are complete
