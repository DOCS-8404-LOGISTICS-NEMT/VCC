## Count-It Rule

Count a path toward the August 9 funding target only if the file contains dated written proof showing:

1. Exact source of funds or vehicle capacity.
2. Amount, payment timing, and permitted use.
3. Conditions, restrictions, repayment, recognition, or reimbursement terms.
4. Whether credit pull, guaranty, lien, deposit, insurance bind, filing, or contract approval is required.
5. CEO/officer approval path before any obligation moves.

Do not count:

- Public listings without vendor confirmation.
- Conversations without amount, timing, and permitted-use terms.
- Grant applications before award or usable reimbursement authority.
- Financing conversations without preapproval, term sheet, or lender timing.
- Sponsor interest without signed pledge, payment record, or approved readiness commitment.

## Funding Stack Order

1. Rental bridge support: fastest path to physical control for dry runs, route timing, partner demonstrations, and vehicle-fit proof.
2. Sponsor/readiness commitment: fastest non-debt cash if a local employer, clinic, county-adjacent partner, or civic sponsor signs a permitted-use pledge.
3. Anchor readiness fee: strongest revenue signal if a clinic, county, public-health, senior, disability, or employer partner pays for readiness and reporting before ride launch.
4. Lease preapproval: best mid-month vehicle possession path if credit, insurance, authority, inventory, and delivery align.
5. Used purchase financing: viable only with quote, down payment, insurance, title/inspection, and delivery timing.
6. SBA/WyoMicro-style microloan: useful backup and startup capital path, but do not count it as one-week cash until an intermediary confirms timing.
7. Grant lane: pursue for scale-up, match, coordinated access, and sustainability, not August 9 cash.
