## Mid-Month Vehicle Strategy

### Lane 1: Dealer accessible van purchase or lease

Use when a dealer has inventory, financing support, and service records available immediately.

Required before deposit:

- Written quote with VIN, mileage, conversion type, ramp/lift type, securement system, title/brand status, warranty/service detail, and delivery timing.
- Independent or dealer inspection of lift/ramp, securement, brakes, tires, HVAC, battery/electrical, lights, doors, and safety equipment.
- Insurance broker confirmation that the vehicle can be insured for the intended Wheatland scheduled passenger service model.
- Entity/title decision: VCC, DOCS 8404, or approved lease/subcontract structure.
- Officer/board approval with price ceiling, down payment, financing ceiling, and authorized signer.

### Lane 2: Short-term accessible rental or lease

Use when VCC needs possession for dry runs, partner demonstrations, route checks, and launch-readiness proof while purchase financing is pending.

Required before rental:

- Confirm commercial/passenger use permissions in the rental or lease agreement.
- Confirm whether the rental provider allows use for any compensated or partner-referred service.
- Confirm insurance coverage, driver qualifications, geographic limits, mileage charges, and damage responsibility.
- Do not transport paid riders unless operating authority, insurance, driver, and policy gates are complete.

### Lane 3: Approved partner vehicle access

Use when an existing provider has compliant vehicle capacity and VCC can operate as a coordinated partner, contractor, dispatcher, sponsor, or funding/development partner.

Required before partner use:

- Written MOU or service agreement.
- Proof of partner insurance, authority, vehicle inspection, driver qualification, and permitted service scope.
- Clear role split for dispatch, rider intake, data, billing, incidents, payment, and liability.
- No representation that VCC operates the vehicle unless the contract and insurance support that statement.

## Vehicle Must-Have Specification

The Wheatland pilot vehicle should be one accessible passenger van or equivalent partner capacity with:

- Wheelchair ramp or lift.
- Wheelchair securement system and passenger restraints.
- Safe entry height and interior layout for the intended rider profile.
- Working heat/air conditioning suitable for Wyoming operations.
- Clean, documented maintenance condition.
- Winter traction plan.
- Backup camera or equivalent safety support.
- Fire extinguisher, first aid kit, reflective triangles, flashlight, cleaning supplies, and daily inspection forms.
- Driver training for passenger assistance and wheelchair securement before any wheelchair rider commitment.

- WYDOT taxi/shuttle guidance states Wyoming taxi and shuttle services transporting persons for hire are required to obtain Commercial Contract Motor Carrier Operating Authority: https://www.dot.state.wy.us/home/vehicle_bus_regulation/taxi_shuttle_service.html
- WYDOT Grant Management states Section 5310 is used for accessible vehicles/equipment for elderly and persons with disabilities, is solicited from private nonprofits and public bodies, and runs through WYDOT BlackCat: https://www.dot.state.wy.us/home/planning_projects/transportation_programs/transit-in-wyoming/grant-management.html
- USDOT ICAM is open with a September 9, 2026 close date and supports coordinated transportation and NEMT-access capital projects: https://www.transportation.gov/rural/grant-toolkit/innovative-coordinated-access-and-mobility-icam-pilot-program

- Wyoming Medicaid Provider Services contact should be used to confirm provider enrollment and billing path before assuming Medicaid revenue: https://health.wyo.gov/healthcarefin/medicaid/for-healthcare-providers/
