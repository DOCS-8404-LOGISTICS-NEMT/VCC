# Grant Calendar And Fastest Funding Path

Draft status: update dates before submission or outreach.

## Funding Strategy

Do not wait for a grant award to start the pilot build. Use grants to support and scale the pilot, but use partner commitments and sponsorships to create the fastest path to a funded launch.

## Open Workstreams

### Anchor Contract

Target:

- Hospital
- Clinic
- Dialysis center
- County public health
- Senior center
- Disability provider
- VSO
- Employer

Offer:

- 90-day readiness fee
- Reserved ride windows
- Per-trip rate
- Weekly reporting
- Limited service area
- Safety/compliance launch gate

### Sponsorship

Sponsor packages:

- $1,000 - Community ride block
- $2,500 - Senior/disability access sponsor
- $5,000 - Healthcare access sponsor
- $10,000 - Vehicle readiness sponsor
- $25,000 - Founding mobility sponsor

Deliverables:

- Sponsor recognition
- Trip impact report
- Service-area report
- Community outreach day
- Vehicle wrap/logo option if approved and compliant

### WYCF

Action:

- Confirm current deadline and applicant eligibility.
- Identify nonprofit/community partner if VCC cannot apply directly.
- Build request around rural access, disability mobility, senior access, veteran support, and community health.

### FTA ICAM

Action:

- Treat as the most urgent active federal opportunity if VCC can assemble an eligible application path before September 9, 2026.
- Build the concept around coordinated access, NEMT coordination, older adults, people with disabilities, low-income riders, one-call/one-click intake, mobility management, dispatch technology, vehicle readiness, and partner referrals.
- Confirm whether VCC may apply directly as a transportation provider/operator or whether a Wyoming public/nonprofit/transit lead is stronger.
- Secure partner letters immediately.

### FHWA ATTAIN

Action:

- Treat as selective because the August 11, 2026 deadline is close and the program is technology-heavy.
- Use only if a government, transit, or transportation-technology lead is ready to include VCC in a rural mobility/dispatch/access proposal.
- Do not divert core launch work unless a qualified lead partner is available now.

### WYDOT/FTA

Action:

- Contact WYDOT Transit staff.
- Ask about 5310, 5311, 5339, coordinated planning, BlackCat, eligible applicants, private operator/subrecipient path, match, vehicle capital, and next cycle.

### VA HRTG

Action:

- Do not present VCC as direct applicant unless eligibility is confirmed.
- Target qualified VSO/SVSA lead.
- Prepare operator/subrecipient MOU, vehicle/driver/insurance plan, reporting plan, and rural veteran transportation need statement.

## Documents Needed For Funding

- Executive summary
- Capability statement
- 90-day budget
- 12-month forecast
- Partner LOI/MOU
- Sponsor package
- Source-proof entity records
- SAM/UEI record
- Insurance quote
- Vehicle plan
- Operating authority path
- Driver and safety plan
- Impact metrics
- Letters of support

## Fastest Path

1. Pick one pilot county/service area.
2. Package one paid 90-day offer.
3. Contact 20 partner targets and 20 sponsor targets.
4. Secure one LOI/MOU or sponsor pledge.
5. Use that commitment to support insurance, vehicle, and operating authority steps.
6. Use launch readiness documents and partner evidence for grants and lender discussions.
