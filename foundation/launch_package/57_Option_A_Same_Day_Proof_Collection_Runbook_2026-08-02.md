## Minimum Proof Fields

### WYDOT

- Required authority type or no-authority explanation.
- MC-100 requirement.
- Form E requirement.
- Passenger-capacity or GVWR trigger.
- USDOT trigger.
- Registration, marking, and legal-name requirements.
- What VCC may do before authority issues.
- Contact name, date/time, phone/email, and follow-up path.

### Insurance

- Whether Wyoming scheduled non-emergency passenger service is eligible.
- Whether NEMT/livery classification applies.
- Whether Form E can be supported.
- Required limits and coverage lines.
- Driver, MVR, age, training, background-check, and experience requirements.
- Rental, lease, partner-vehicle, or owned-vehicle treatment.
- Required vehicle details for a firm quote.
- Exclusions or no-fit reasons.

### Vehicle / Rental / Lease

- Vehicle class, VIN/stock if available, entry type, ramp/lift, securement.
- August 10-15 availability or earliest alternative date.
- Total due, deposit requirement, cancellation terms.
- Business-use permission.
- Insurance requirements.
- Pickup/delivery location and timing.
- Whether dry runs and partner demonstrations are permitted.
- Whether compensated passenger service is restricted or allowed only after insurance/authority.

### Financing / Funding

- Amount range or pledge/readiness-support path.
- Expected timeline.
- Required documents.
- Credit-pull, application, guaranty, lien, or collateral trigger.
- Whether vehicle quote, insurance response, or sponsor/partner letters strengthen the file.
- Whether funds can realistically affect the August 9 decision gate.

## Same-Day Recommendation Rule

Recommend `CONTINUE - rental bridge` if:

- At least one rental vehicle can be available by August 15.
- Insurance path is not blocked.
- Authority path allows dry runs/demos or gives a path to paid service.
- Funding proof is moving or bridge authorization can be considered.

Recommend `CONTINUE - purchase/lease` if:

- A specific vehicle or lease unit is available.
- Total due and timing are written or captured in dated call notes.
- Insurance path is binder-ready or close.
- Financing/preapproval or bridge funds can close before the vehicle is lost.

Recommend `PIVOT - partner vehicle` if:

- VCC cannot insure, rent, lease, or purchase fast enough.
- A local partner can supply compliant vehicle capacity under a written role.

Recommend `HOLD` if:

- No credible vehicle path exists.
- Insurance or authority path is blocked.
- Funding proof is absent by August 9.
- Any vendor, lender, insurer, agency, sponsor, or partner requires an obligation before approval.
